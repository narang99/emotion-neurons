# Sparse neuron sets for the layer-25 emotion writes, and what the top 64 do

Run 2026-11-18. Configs `configs/top64_l25.yaml` (selection, harvest, modes) and `configs/sparse_l25.yaml` (size /
direction trade-off). Outputs: `results/top64_l25/`, `results/sparse_l25_{raw,denoised}/`, and
`s3://narang99-private/emotion-neurons/top64_l25/` (activation memmap).


## Setting

- The MLP's emotion write splits exactly over neurons: `delta_e = sum_n u_n`, `u_n = W_down[:, n] * dA_e[n]`.
  - `dA_e[n]`: neuron n's mean activation on emotion e's train stories minus its mean over the 12 emotions.
  - Rebuild error 0.07–0.12% (bf16).
- Keeping a set S = every other neuron held at its 12-emotion mean; the write becomes `sum_{n in S} u_n`.
- Metrics:
  - **cos**: with `delta_e` on the train stories (the reconstruction);
  - **transfer**: the same S with held-out activations (20 stories per emotion) against the train write. Noise is
    independent, so fitting story noise earns nothing. The full set's transfer is the reference (0.56–0.88).
  - A first held-out metric (S's held-out write against the held-out write) was dropped: both share the held-out
    noise of S's neurons, which inflates it with k.

## Log

- **Round 1: what is the smallest set keeping the direction?** Greedy forward selection (each step adds the neuron
  that raises cos most): 703–1048 neurons for cos 0.9, 1418–1910 for 0.95; 0.99 not reached in 2000.
  - Random sets need 8727–10584 for 0.9; ordering by projection c_n needs 1567–5635; by |u_n| 1414–2224.
  - Top 64 per emotion: cos 0.55–0.68. Top 8: 0.29–0.51.
- **Round 2: do swaps beat greedy?** Barely. Best-improvement 1-swaps at fixed k, and shrinking (drop the cheapest
  neuron, swap back above tau) save 0–3%: cos 0.9 at 700–1032 instead of 702–1046. Greedy is a 1-swap local optimum.
- **Round 3: does the set generalise?** Yes, and a sparse set generalises as well as the whole MLP.
  - Transfer of the full write: 0.56 (calm) to 0.88 (inspired).
  - Greedy reaches it at k = 298 (happy), 314 (proud), 441 (calm), 628–957 (guilty, loving, sad, surprised,
    desperate, nervous, afraid), 1229–1233 (inspired, angry). Train cos at that k: 0.75–0.93.
  - It then exceeds it: best greedy transfer 0.63–0.90 (e.g. happy 0.81 at 2233 vs 0.72 full). The other ~10k
    neurons add story noise.
  - At train cos 0.9 (shrunk sets): transfer 0.61–0.85; random 1024-neuron sets 0.09–0.27.
- **Round 4: can selection avoid fitting noise?** Slightly. Neurons needed to match the full-set transfer,
  summed over 12 emotions:

  | selection | raw target | denoised target |
  |---|---|---|
  | greedy | 8968 | 8736 |
  | crossfit (alignment across halves of the train stories) | 8918 | — |
  | shrunk, kappa 1 (target leans on reproducible shifts, `1 - 1/t^2`) | 8747 (8/12 emotions better) | 8171 (10/12) |
  | shrunk, kappa 4 / 9 | worse; kappa 9 never matches for 6–7 emotions | |
  | by projection c_n | worse (341–2484 per emotion; never for inspired) | |

  - The gains (2.5–6.5%) are within the noise of a 20-story held-out set; kappa was not tuned (tuning on the
    held-out would leak).
  - First crossfit version used the full-train norm with a cross-half alignment: neurons near zero on the full set
    but of opposite sign in the halves got huge scores; transfer ~0. Fixed by using the halves' own norms.
- **Round 5: denoised target** (neutral PCs of the write projected out of `delta_e` and every `u_n`): same picture;
  match sizes 268–1401, cos 0.9 at 729–1065.
- **No knee.** Train cos and transfer rise roughly linearly in log k for every emotion (`tradeoff.png`): there is no
  small core that carries the write. The useful size is set by noise: 300–1200 neurons (2–10% of 12288).
- **Chosen sets** (`results/sparse_l25_{raw,denoised}/sets.json`): per emotion, the greedy prefix that matches the
  full-set transfer, and the smallest shrunk set with train cos >= 0.9.

## Top 64: what they do

- **Selection:** round-robin down the 12 greedy orders, ranks 1–8 per emotion. 64 neurons, 93 (neuron, emotion)
  pairs; 9964 and 10272 are picked for 5 and 7 emotions.
- **Harvest / modes:** 2,000,432 tokens; median 4 modes per neuron, median 23% of hits unclustered; activation
  check recomputed 0.722 vs harvested 0.723.
- **Haiku quality:** checked 6561 against its snippets; every mode name matched. Sonnet not needed.
- **Emotion share** (hits in modes Haiku labels with any emotion; same prompt for the 20 random controls of
  stage3_l25):

  | group | neurons | mean share | neurons with >= 50% |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | best greedy rank 1–2 | 17 | 0.42 | |
  | best greedy rank 3–5 | 24 | 0.23 | |
  | best greedy rank 6–8 | 23 | 0.19 | |
  | all 64 | 64 | 0.27 | 12 |
  | random controls | 20 | 0.16 | 3 |

- **Own-emotion modes are rare:** 1 of 93 pairs has >= 50% of hits in a mode labelled with its own emotion
  (3068 proud: "proud" after "was very/so", 98%). Most emotion modes are "emotion-general".
- **Examples (share of p97+ hits):**
  - 3068 (proud #2): "proud" itself after "was very"/"was so", 98%.
  - 11109 (afraid #1, nervous #3, sad #2, angry #5): the token after "felt"/"was" naming fear or anxiety, 98%.
  - 3361 (happy #8, proud #7): feeling adjectives after "was"/"feeling" (nervous, brave, scared), 98%.
  - 48 (guilty #1): "and" after a child's feeling word ("felt bad and", "ashamed and"), 96%.
  - 5869 (desperate #4): "and" linking a feeling to the next action ("was sad and didn't understand"), 97%.
  - 9904 (inspired #3): period or "excited"/"happy" after a joyful reaction, 95%.
  - 8776 (desperate #1): the subject after a failed search ("could not find Fluffy. She looked"), 69%. A
    lost-and-found plot neuron.
  - 4311 (surprised #1): "then"/"and" after a character's reaction, 88%; 84% of hits in TinyStories.
  - 10272 (loving #1, happy #2, + 5 more): clause starts in playground scenes, 95%; story structure.
  - 9964 (inspired/calm/sad #1): last word of a positive phrase ("smiled with pride"), 96%, but carries only
    +0.010–0.016 of `|delta_e|` along it: it is picked for cancelling off-direction parts of others' writes.
- **Not emotion-related at all:** 6561 (chat-transcript structure), 7555 (88% FineWeb essays), 1637, 6512, 155.
  Several of these sit at ranks 6–8.

## Surprises

- **Greedy picks cancellers.** For inspired, picks 2–8 carry +0.000 to +0.002 of `|delta_e|` along it (pick 1, +0.010).
  Inspired's write has outlier dims (2276, 549, 1838); its top picks mostly remove off-direction error.
- **The emotion modes are generic.** The top writers fire around feeling words of any kind, not their emotion's
  word; the emotion-specific part sits in which feeling-word contexts dominate each emotion's stories.
