Happy neurons: what they fire on, at both poles

The first 532 neurons of the layer-25 happy greedy set (75% of the happy write) and 20 random controls, harvested over 2M tokens of general text (fineweb-edu, TinyStories, UltraChat). Each pole's hits (p97 and above, p3 and below) are clustered on the MLP input. Two models judged every cluster blind: a short name, and how many of its windows sit in a happy scene (scene, not word). Happy side is the pole happy moves the neuron toward: high when its happy shift is positive, low when negative. A neuron's class comes from the clusters on its happy side: happy if both judges call one of them happy (3+ of 5 windows), split if only one judge does, not happy if neither does. "Not happy" neurons push toward happy while firing on something else.
